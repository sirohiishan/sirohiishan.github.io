What I Bring

  1. I start with the unhappy path.

    Translation: I will ask “but what if there’s no signal?” in every single review. Sorry in advance.

  2. I bring a prototype, not a pitch.

    Clicking a real thing beats a 45 minute call about imagining one.

  3. I build the system before the screens.

    30+ components, every layer named. Yes, every single one. Yes, I’m that person.

  4. I finish what I start.

    When the other designer left mid project, I took it all the way to handoff. Alone. Dramatically.
