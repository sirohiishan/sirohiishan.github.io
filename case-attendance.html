Overview

This was a change request to a live audit app. Reviewers couldn't tell when field auditors arrived, how long they stayed, or if they were on site at all. In three days, I designed geotagged punch in and punch out that wraps around the existing audit flow instead of rebuilding it. Auditors are never blocked, and anything unusual is flagged for review.

lockUnder NDA. The client is scribbled out like this , and the screens are wireframes I rebuilt with fictional data, not the shipped design. The thinking, flows and decisions are real.

Who Uses the App

A facility that wants to be certified gets an in-person visit from a field auditor. Over one or more days, the auditor checks its machinery, processes and everything else the certification requires, and fills in the findings on a mobile app. A reviewing officer then goes through those findings and decides whether the facility gets certified. This change request was for the field auditor's app.

The Problem

Field auditors are paid for their visits, so the client needed to know how long each one actually worked on site. The app tracked the audit, but not the auditor. Location was asked once and never used as evidence. They wanted a simple attendance system, where auditors punch in when they arrive and punch out when they leave.

visibility_off What reviewers couldn't see
  • When auditors arrived
  • How long they stayed
  • Whether they were on site at all
forum What was asked
  • Add punch in and punch out, so we know how long auditors work on site.
  • And a periodic check that auditors are still there.

Constraints

The request came in mid-project, on a flow auditors were already using.

  • timer3 days

    A hard deadline, so reuse wherever possible.

  • do_not_disturb_onA live flow

    Sections, saving and submit already worked. Wrap them, don't rewire them.

  • alt_routeMessy edge cases

    Denied permissions, wrong places, forgotten punch outs and multi-day visits.

The Solution

How attendance fits into the audit, each new moment in the order the auditor meets it, through to verification and history.

01New Flow and Edge Cases

Attendance wraps around the audit. The sections in the middle don't change, and when a visit doesn't go to plan, the auditor is never blocked.

02Punching In Before the Audit Starts

Start audit still unlocks by date. The first time, the app asks for location. Then the auditor punches in with a selfie, and sees the location and time stamped on it before it uploads.

03During the Audit, the Clock Stays on Top

The section screens didn't change. A slim duration bar shows time on site, and punch out stays pinned to the bottom. If the auditor leaves without punching out, the overview shows the active session and a way back in.

04Punching Out, Then Submitting

Punching out uses the same selfie flow. Submit unlocks only once the auditor has punched out and every required section is complete. If they forget to punch out, the system does it at the end of the day.

05Verification and History

The ask also wanted proof that auditors stayed on site. At set intervals, the app asks for a quick verification selfie, and a day by day history shows every punch in, verification and auto punch out. Both shipped.

Key Decisions

Five small calls that kept a three day change safe to ship.

  1. flag01
    Flag, don't block

    A weak GPS signal shouldn't fail an audit.

  2. layers02
    Wrap the audit, don't rebuild it

    Three days, on a flow auditors already knew.

  3. lock_open03
    Punch out unlocks submit

    Every audit ends with a closed attendance record.

  4. photo_camera04
    Use the native camera

    Familiar to auditors, faster to build.

  5. repeat05
    Sessions, not shifts

    Auditors leave, come back, and sometimes forget to punch out.

The Outcome

Every state and edge case was handed off within the three days. The project is ongoing, so no numbers yet. Here's what I'd track.

  • scheduleAuto punch out rate

    If it's high, punch out needs to be easier to spot.

  • flagLocation flags

    How many are real issues, not GPS noise.

  • timerTime to first section

    Punch in should add seconds, not minutes.

Short deadlines reward reuse. The real work was deciding what not to touch.
