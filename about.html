Things You Were Going to Ask Anyway

The interview, except I went first.

  1. You, probablySo, when can you start?

    IshanWhen do I get to say ‘we’ instead of ‘you’?

  2. You, probablyUI designer or UX designer?

    IshanUI is where I'm strongest. I still map the flow before the pixels, because a pretty screen on a broken flow is just broken in high resolution.

  3. You, probablyWill developers like you?

    IshanReal components, every layer named, and a prototype they can click. So far nobody has thrown a laptop at me.

  4. You, probablyAre you scared of AI taking your job?

    IshanI used it to build and ship my own iOS app. We're on speaking terms.

  5. You, probablyWhat happens when a project goes sideways?

    IshanI finish it anyway. When the other designer left mid project, I took it to handoff on my own.

  6. You, probablyBiggest weakness? And don't say perfectionism.

    IshanGoing quiet and solo for too long. A capstone taught me that the hard way, so now I show work early, even when it's ugly.

  7. You, probablyWhat do you do when you're not designing?

    IshanHonestly, very little, and that's on purpose. I binge a show, step out for a while, put some music on and give my head a quiet break. That's how I come back with fresh eyes.

  8. You, probablyWhy should we hire you?

    IshanYou read eight answers from a stranger on the internet. I think we'd work well together. Say hi.
