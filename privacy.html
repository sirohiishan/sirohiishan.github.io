Privacy

A simple portfolio. Here’s how information is handled.

Last updated 24 October 2026

Who runs this site

This portfolio is operated by Ishan Sirohi in India. For privacy questions or requests, email sirohi.ishan@icloud.com.

Visiting the portfolio

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub receives the information needed to deliver and secure the website and logs visitors’ IP addresses for security, including when they are not signed in. Read GitHub’s Pages information and Privacy Statement for details.

I do not add analytics, advertising trackers or optional tracking cookies. Fonts and media are served with the site. There is no contact form or visitor account system.

When you contact me

If you email me, I receive your email address, message and any information you choose to include. I use it to respond and manage the discussion, including job opportunities and recruitment enquiries. Messages to this site’s iCloud address are handled through iCloud Mail. Read Apple’s Privacy Policy for its processing practices.

Where GDPR applies, I rely on my legitimate interest in responding to job-related and other professional correspondence. Please avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information.

Retention and international processing

I keep correspondence while it is reasonably needed for the enquiry, the relevant professional relationship and applicable legal obligations, and delete it when it is no longer needed. This is based on the purpose and context of the discussion rather than one fixed period for every message.

GitHub and Apple have their own retention policies and may process information outside your country, including in the United States. Their privacy policies explain their international processing and safeguards. I do not control their infrastructure logs or promise a fixed deletion period for those records.

Your choices and rights

You can email me to ask about information I hold about you or request access, correction or deletion. Depending on the law that applies, you may also have rights to restrict processing, object, receive portable data or complain to your data protection authority. I respond in accordance with applicable law.

For information GitHub or Apple handles as a separate controller, their privacy policies explain how to contact them.

External links and updates

External websites, app stores and professional profiles linked from this portfolio have their own privacy practices. Those apply when you choose to visit them.

I will update this notice if the way the portfolio handles information changes.
